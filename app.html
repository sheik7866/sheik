Working demo

Compare your shop.

Pick your supermarkets, then search for a product or type a barcode. Grocer prices the item across your stores, keeps a shopping list and adds up which shop is cheapest for the whole basket.

Scanned barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, and searches also query Grocer's public product catalogue, so names, brands, sizes and store coverage are real. Grocer doesn't publish prices, so prices are estimates — except the PAK'nSAVE and New World shelf prices checked every night. Switch to “All products” to browse the full catalogue pulled from those two stores. Nothing here is live supermarket pricing.
