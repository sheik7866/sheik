Why Grocer

One list. Every price. No guessing.

Features

Everything you need in the aisle.

Built for the way people actually shop: quick checks, a running list, and a clear answer about which store to walk into.

Compare stores

Pick the supermarkets near you and see every price side by side — up to six stores on Free, thirty on Pro.

Scan a barcode

Point your camera at a product and price it while you're standing in front of the shelf — no typing required.

Price history

See how a price has moved over months — or years on Pro — so you know a genuine markdown when you see one.

Lists that travel

One shopping list, synced across your phone, tablet and the web app, so the list at home is the list in your hand.

Whole-basket totals

Grocer adds up your list store by store, so you can compare a full shop — or split it across two stores for the specials.

Independent, and no ads

Grocer is built by one Kiwi developer and funded by Pro subscriptions rather than banner ads or sponsored placements.

How it works

Three steps, one cheaper shop.

  1. 01

    Choose your stores

    Select the supermarkets you actually shop at — your local branch, not a national average.

  2. 02

    Scan or search

    Scan a barcode or search by name. Grocer matches the product across your stores and shows the price of each.

  3. 03

    Shop where it's cheapest

    Take the list to the winning store, or cherry-pick the best specials at two. Either way, you go in informed.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Grocer free?

Yes. The free plan covers comparing prices across up to six stores, a shopping list, barcode scanning, search and three months of price history. Pro is an optional subscription for people who want more stores, unlimited lists and the full price history.

Which supermarkets can I compare?

Grocer compares prices from New Zealand's major supermarkets, including Woolworths, New World and Pak'nSave, along with the other stores available in the app. You choose which ones appear in your comparisons.

How does the Pro subscription work?

Pro is account-based, so signing in keeps your subscription attached to you across devices and platforms. New subscribers get a one-month free trial and can cancel anytime through the App Store or Google Play.

Does Grocer work on iPhone and Android?

It does. Grocer is available on the App Store and Google Play, and there's a web app at grocer.nz if you'd rather shop from a browser.

Do I need an account?

You can use the core features as a guest. An account is what syncs your lists across devices and carries your Pro subscription.

How current are the prices?

Prices are collected from the supermarkets and refreshed regularly, but they can change store to store and week to week. Treat Grocer as a very good guide, then check the shelf label.

Who makes Grocer?

Grocer is an independent app built in New Zealand by developer Roc Wong. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at info@grocer.nz or in the Discord community.
