Pro FAQ

Billing, plainly.

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, so billing and cancellation stay where you expect them.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. New subscribers get one month of Pro at no charge. If you cancel before the trial ends you won't be billed, and the Free plan keeps working.

How do I cancel?

Cancel through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, at any time. Your Pro access continues until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Can I use Pro on more than one device?

Pro is account-based, so signing in on another phone, tablet or the web app carries your subscription with you.

What happens if I go back to Free?

Your account stays and the app keeps working. You'll be back to six stores, one list and three months of price history.

Where can I buy Pro?

Inside the Trolleybird app on the App Store or Google Play, from the Pro screen. You'll need an account so the subscription follows you between devices.
