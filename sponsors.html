Thank you

Three years of Pro, on us.

As a token of appreciation, all past and current sponsors can claim three years of Grocer Pro membership for free.

  1. Open your Grocer account page and copy your User ID.
  2. Email info@grocer.nz with that User ID and your GitHub username.
  3. We'll activate your Pro membership on your account.

A note to current sponsors

You can stop sponsoring.

Grocer is transitioning to a self-sustaining model through the premium plan, so ongoing sponsorship isn't needed to keep the lights on. If you supported Grocer through sponsorship, feel free to cancel it — and please claim the Pro membership you've earned.
